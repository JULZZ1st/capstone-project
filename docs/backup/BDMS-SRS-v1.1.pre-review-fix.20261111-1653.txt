1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the implemented and intended requirements for the Barangay Donation Management System (BDMS), a web application for recording donations, managing inventory, processing fund requests and disbursements, reconciling bank activity, and producing authorized reports for the barangay.

This Version 1.1 reflects the current draft scope and Laravel implementation. It is prepared for adviser and panel review and is not approved. A requirement is included only when the system implements it or when it is clearly marked as planned or requiring verification.

1.2 Scope

BDMS provides authenticated access based on exactly two roles: Admin and Staff. Staff records donors, contributions, inventory, Fund Requests, voucher documents, payments, Bank Reconciliation work, and authorized reports. Admin manages Staff accounts and reviews or decides restricted cash, Fund Request, voucher, and reconciliation actions.

Distribution Management is outside the approved scope. The system does not manage beneficiary distribution events, distribution schedules, distribution approvals, or distribution reports. Inventory stock-out remains in scope as an inventory movement or adjustment and must not be described as beneficiary distribution.

The current implementation does not expose a public donor self-service contribution route. Donors are recorded by authorized staff through the authenticated Donation Portal; review, inventory, financial, reconciliation, and reporting actions remain role- and workspace-controlled.

2. Overall Description

2.1 Product Perspective 

BDMS is a Laravel web application using Blade views, Eloquent models, authorization policies, a MySQL database, and application services that save related records together. It replaces fragmented manual records with a shared workspace for donations, inventory, financial requests, vouchers, reconciliation, reports, and system activity history.

A valid in-kind contribution is saved with its receipt information and inventory posting in one database transaction. Stock movements retain quantity before and after values and may identify the originating contribution item. Cash contribution verification, fund request approval, voucher processing, and reconciliation use controlled workflow states and role checks.

2.2 Product Functions

The principal functions are authentication and role management; donor and contribution management; automatic in-kind inventory posting; inventory stock-in, stock-out, and adjustment; fund request and disbursement voucher processing; bank reconciliation and discrepancy resolution; reports and exports; and security activity auditing. Beneficiary Distribution Management is not a product function.

2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints

The system shall use server-side authorization in addition to UI visibility; database writes for multi-record workflows shall be transactional; evidence files shall be stored and served through protected application paths; migrations shall be additive for applied environments; and the application shall preserve historical records and audit-relevant source links. The implementation shall not introduce Distribution Management functionality.

2.6 Assumptions and Dependencies

The installation has a configured MySQL database, writable storage for evidence, and an Admin who manages Staff accounts. No notification requirement is in scope. Existing Staff accounts may remain during transition. Historical records imported before inventory linking may not have a linked source and are not automatically backfilled.

2.7 Apportioning of Requirements

The current release covers the requirements in Section 3 except those marked Partially Implemented, Implemented with limitation, Not Implemented, or Needs Verification in Appendix B. Future enhancements must not be presented as implemented requirements until verified.

3.2.6 Fund Requests and Disbursement Vouchers

Fund Request workflow:

1. Staff creates and submits a Pending request.

2. Staff prepares the Disbursement Voucher and records the required certification.

3. Staff uploads the signed voucher and supporting documents.

4. Admin reviews and approves or rejects with a written reason.

5. If rejected, the required workflow is for Staff to read the reason, edit the same request, add missing information or documents, and resubmit it.

6. Admin reviews the resubmitted request again.

7. Admin approval does not release payment.

8. Staff records actual payment separately after approval.

9. A voided request remains void and its history is retained.

Current code does not yet allow ordinary editing and resubmission after rejection.

3.2.7 Bank Reconciliation

Bank Reconciliation workflow: Staff creates a period, imports a CSV or XLSX statement, records or matches bank movements, and reports unmatched transactions as discrepancies. Admin reviews the report and may accept the explanation or request correction. Staff follows the existing update and resolution steps. Accepting an explanation does not match the transaction automatically. A period is submitted only after the balance, matching, timing-item, and unresolved-discrepancy checks pass; Admin then approves and locks, rejects back to Draft, or reopens the period with a reason.

Must

Known limitations and current implementation status are recorded here so intended requirements remain separate from verified application behavior.

Cash review: ContributionPolicy and the verify endpoint still allow Staff to verify a pending cash donation created by another Staff user. This conflicts with FR-CSH-03 and FR-CSH-04; Appendix B marks the affected requirements Partially Implemented.

Cash rejection reason: the current rejection path changes the contribution status but does not provide a dedicated required rejection-reason field. FR-CSH-03 therefore remains Partially Implemented until the required reason is captured and preserved.

Rejected Fund Requests retain rejection reasons and history, but ordinary update is limited to Pending requests. Correction editing and resubmission of the same request are required future behavior and are not implemented.

Signed voucher replacement is allowed while a request is Pending or Approved. Replacement after approval does not trigger a new Admin review, and approval does not require an approved signed-voucher review status before payment.

Bank reconciliation: Admin acceptance of a discrepancy explanation does not create a match. A valid timing item may remain unresolved only when the code rules are satisfied; other unresolved discrepancies and any bank ledger entries still marked unmatched or unreconciled block submission or approval.

Posted in-kind stock is not reversed by the current cash-only void action. Any correction process for posted in-kind quantities remains an adviser or barangay decision and must preserve the original movement history.

Campaigns is retained as a legacy migration/model item and is not used by the current donation workflow. Distribution Management remains out of scope.

Audit visibility: the shared authenticated route group and report controller allow both Admin and Staff to view the audit report. No role may edit or delete audit activity through the application. Authentication and every report-export event are not represented as one uniform audit event across all modules.

Appendix A. Current Entity Relationship Overview

Migration-backed relationship map: roles have many users and workspace memberships. Workspaces scope donors, contributions, inventory, disbursements, and reconciliation records through workspace_id. Donors have many contributions; contributions have items and evidence; contribution items may link to inventory items; stock movements may link to source contribution items. Disbursement vouchers and payments belong to Fund Requests. Bank reconciliation periods have discrepancies and imported bank entries. Campaigns is retained as a legacy migration/model item and is not used by the current donation workflow. No Distribution Management entity is in scope.

The schema intentionally does not contain a Distribution Management relationship. Inventory stock-out is represented by an inventory movement and is not a beneficiary-distribution record.
