Admin

- Approve or reject cash disbursement requests
- Cancel incorrect cash transactions with a recorded reason
- Manage staff access
- Review audit logs
- Manage categories and units

Secretary

- Create and update donor records
- Record donations
- Record stock-in and stock-out
- Maintain inventory records

Treasurer

- Verify cash donations
- Record or import bank transactions
- Compare bank withdrawals/deposits with system cash records
- Reconcile matching transactions
- Review financial ledger and reports
